COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I stop appearing in donor searches?
Open the app > About > Your Account > Hide my donor listing. You disappear from searches immediately. Your account stays active and you can list again whenever you want.

How do I delete my account completely?
Open the app > About > Your Account > Delete my account. You will be asked for your password to confirm. This permanently removes your profile and your donor listing. It cannot be undone.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, type your email address and tap "Forgot password?". We will email you a reset link. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Why do I have to sign in just to search for donors?
Because donor listings contain personal phone numbers. Requiring an account means numbers cannot be collected anonymously in bulk, and it gives us an account to suspend if someone misuses them.

Someone is calling me for something other than blood donation.
Tap "Report listing" on their entry, or email us. Then hide your own listing if you want the calls to stop straight away. Never send money to anyone who contacts you through this app.

Is a donor shown in the app medically safe to receive blood from?
We cannot tell you that. Every detail in a listing is self-reported and is not verified or medically screened by us. All blood must be collected and tested at a licensed blood bank, which performs the required screening. Use this app to find a willing donor — never to arrange a private transfusion.

Do you charge anything?
No. The app is free and non-commercial. Blood is never to be bought or sold — selling blood is illegal in India. If anyone asks you to pay for blood, report them to us and refuse.


FRAUD WARNING

Nobody from Bharath Blood Donor will ever ask you for money, for your password, or for an OTP. If someone contacts you claiming to be from us and asks for any of these, they are lying. Report it to us at bharathblooddonor@gmail.com.
