DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT AND DATA
Bharath Blood Donor

Last updated: 26 July 2026


This page explains how to delete your Bharath Blood Donor account and what
happens to your data when you do.


HOW TO DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT FROM INSIDE THE APP

This is the fastest way and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Bharath Blood Donor app and sign in.
  2. Tap "About" in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the "Your Account" section.
  4. Tap "Delete my account".
  5. Enter your password to confirm it is really you.
  6. Confirm the warning. Your account is deleted straight away.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account, and you will need
to register again from scratch if you want to use the app later.


HOW TO REQUEST DELETION BY EMAIL

If you cannot sign in — for example you have forgotten your password or lost
access to your email address — write to us instead.

  Email: bharathblooddonor@gmail.com
  Subject: Delete my account

Include the email address you registered with, and, if you were listed as a
donor, the phone number on your listing so we can identify the right record.

We will verify that the request genuinely comes from the account holder before
we delete anything, then confirm by email once it is done. We respond within
30 days of receiving your request.


WHAT IS DELETED

When your account is deleted, the following is erased permanently and
immediately:

  - Your login account, including your email address and password hash
  - Your profile: display name, district and blood group
  - Your donor listing in full, if you had one: name, age, gender, blood
    group, district, city, phone number, availability status and last
    donation period

Once deleted, this data is gone from our live systems and you will no longer
appear in any donor search.


WHAT IS KEPT, AND FOR HOW LONG

  - Emergency blood requests you posted are NOT removed automatically when
    you delete your account. A live request may still be helping a patient
    find blood, so we do not withdraw it without being asked. Each request
    contains the patient name, blood group, units needed, hospital name and
    location, contact name and phone number, and the reason for the request.
    If you want your requests removed as well, say so in your deletion email
    and we will delete them too. Otherwise they are removed [YOUR RETENTION
    PERIOD - we recommend 30 days after the date needed].

  - Reports you submitted about abusive or fake listings are kept for
    [YOUR RETENTION PERIOD] so that we can detect repeat offenders. These
    are visible only to our moderation team and are not linked to your
    listing once your account is gone.

  - Routine encrypted backups held by our infrastructure provider, Google
    Firebase, may retain a copy for a short period before they expire. These
    backups are never used to restore a deleted account and are not
    accessible to us for any other purpose.

We keep no other copy of your personal data after deletion.


DELETING YOUR DONOR LISTING WITHOUT DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

If you only want to stop appearing in donor searches but wish to keep your
account, open About, then "Your Account", then tap "Hide my donor listing".
Your listing stops appearing in searches and you can list yourself again at
any time.
