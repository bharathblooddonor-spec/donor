## Description (4000 max)

Bharath Blood Donor connects patients and families in Andhra Pradesh with voluntary blood donors who have chosen to make themselves reachable. The app is free. There are no charges, no subscriptions and no advertising.

SEARCH FOR DONORS

Filter by blood group, district and city to find voluntary donors near you. Every district of Andhra Pradesh is covered, including Vijayawada, Guntur, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati, Nellore, Kurnool, Rajahmundry and Kakinada. All common groups are supported, along with rare types such as Bombay phenotype (Oh+ and Oh-) and Rh-null.

CONTACT DONORS DIRECTLY

Call, message or reach a donor on WhatsApp from inside the app. No middlemen, no brokers and no waiting for someone to pass on your request.

POST AN EMERGENCY REQUEST

Need blood urgently? Post the blood group, units needed, hospital and date, and your request goes to volunteers across the state. Mark it fulfilled once help arrives so nobody calls you unnecessarily.

LIST YOURSELF AS A DONOR

Registering as a donor is optional and takes under a minute. Set your availability status, record when you last donated, and hide your listing whenever you need a break.

CHECK BLOOD GROUP COMPATIBILITY

A built-in compatibility checker shows which groups each blood type can donate to and receive from — useful when the exact group you need is unavailable.

FIND YOUR AREA QUICKLY

Tap once to detect your district and city. Your coordinates are used only to fill in those two fields and are never stored, shared or tracked in the background.

YOUR PRIVACY

Signing in is required before anyone can see donor contact details. This is deliberate: it prevents phone numbers being collected in bulk by anyone who happens to find the app.

Listing yourself as a donor is a separate, explicit choice. When you list, your name, blood group, city and phone number become visible to other signed-in users so they can reach you in an emergency. You can hide your listing or delete your account permanently at any time from the About screen.

We do not sell your data, show advertisements or use third-party trackers.

PLEASE READ

Bharath Blood Donor is not a blood bank and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is a contact directory that helps people reach one another. All donor details are entered by donors themselves and are not medically screened or verified by us. Blood must always be collected, tested and transfused at a licensed blood bank or hospital.

Never pay anyone for blood. Voluntary donation is free, and the sale of blood is illegal in India. If someone asks you for money, do not proceed, and report the listing inside the app.

Donors must be between 18 and 65 years of age.

In a medical emergency, call 108 immediately. Do not wait for a reply through this app.

## Notes (4000 max)

Bharath Blood Donor is a free, non-commercial directory that helps patients in Andhra Pradesh, India contact voluntary blood donors. It is not a blood bank and provides no medical advice or treatment.

HOW TO REVIEW

1. Sign in with the account above.
2. SEARCH tab — choose a blood group, then District "NTR" and City "Vijayawada", and tap Search Blood Donors. Matching donors appear with Call, SMS and WhatsApp buttons.
3. REQUESTS tab — the emergency board. Tap "+ Post Request" to create one, and "Mark Fulfilled" to close it.
4. BE A DONOR tab — optional registration that publishes a listing. Blood group consent is collected here with a separate, unticked checkbox.
5. ABOUT AP tab — blood group compatibility checker, helpline numbers, and Your Account, which contains "Hide my donor listing" and "Delete my account".

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In-app account deletion is at ABOUT AP > Your Account > Delete my account. It asks for the password to re-authenticate, then permanently deletes the profile and donor listing. A public web page describing the same process is linked from our privacy policy.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)

Donor listings and emergency requests are user-submitted. Every listing and request carries a "Report" action, reports are written to a moderation queue we triage, and accounts can be removed. Sign-in is required before any contact details are visible, which is what prevents anonymous bulk collection of phone numbers.

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER (Guideline 1.4.1)

The app never advises on medical matters. Donor details are self-reported and are not medically verified by us. The app states throughout that blood must be collected and tested at a licensed blood bank, that donation is voluntary and unpaid, and that 108 should be called in an emergency.

DATA COLLECTED

Email and password (Firebase Authentication), plus name, age, blood group, district, city and phone number for users who choose to list as donors. Location is requested only when the user taps "Use my current location", is used in memory to identify their district and city, and is never stored or transmitted.

# App Privacy — Data Collection

Tick exactly these seven. Everything else on the page stays unchecked.

| Section | Tick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | **Name** | Account display name, donor name, patient and contact names on requests |
| Contact Info | **Email Address** | Firebase Authentication |
| Contact Info | **Phone Number** | Published on donor listings and emergency requests |
| Health & Fitness | **Health** | Blood group. Your own privacy policy says "Your blood group is health data" |
| Location | **Coarse Location** | District and city are stored on the listing and shown to other users |
| User Content | **Other User Content** | Free text: hospital name, reason for request, abuse report text |
| Identifiers | **User ID** | The Firebase uid, stored on users, donors, requests and reports |

Explicitly NOT collected — leave unchecked:

- Physical Address. A district and city is coarse location, not a postal address.
- Precise Location. Coordinates are read into memory, reverse-geocoded to a
  district, then discarded. Apple does not count data used only to service a
  request in real time, so this is not "collected". See src/utils/location.js.
- Sensitive Info. That bucket is race, sexual orientation, religion, biometrics
  and similar. Health has its own category and blood group belongs there.
- Contacts. The app never reads the address book.
- Photos or Videos. There is no upload and the avatar picker was removed.
- Search History. Searches run as live queries and are never persisted.
- Financial Info, Purchases, Browsing History, Device ID, Usage Data,
  Diagnostics, Surroundings, Body, Other Data. None apply.
- Diagnostics stays unchecked because no crash reporter is wired up. If Sentry
  is ever added, Crash Data must be ticked.

## The follow-up questions, asked once per data type

Apple asks three things about each of the seven. The answers are identical
every time:

1. Used for tracking? **No.** No ads, no analytics, no data brokers, no
   cross-app or cross-site tracking. This is also why no App Tracking
   Transparency prompt is required.
2. Linked to the user's identity? **Yes.** Every one of these is stored against
   the account uid.
3. Purposes? **App Functionality** only. Do not tick Analytics, Third-Party
   Advertising, Developer's Advertising or Marketing, or Product
   Personalisation.
